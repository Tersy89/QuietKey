Enigma 0.7.0 joining protocol — 29 October 2026 (branch feature/0.7.0-messages; not in the app yet)

Added JoinProtocol (QKJ3/QKG3/QKA3 and v3 rosters with introductions). KeyPools now holds
members' reserve keys for the owner. Old backup private keys are kept for 365 days.

Executed: gradlew testDebugUnitTest — 114 JVM unit tests, 0 failures (105 before plus 9 in
JoinProtocolTest).

JoinProtocolTest covers:
- owner and joiner have full protection both ways after joining;
- a later member and an existing member meet through owner introductions, carried in a message,
  with full protection from the first message;
- re-applying the same roster does not reuse introductions;
- an invitation opens only for its own request and device, and tampering fails;
- only the owner invites and signs rosters;
- forged requests and receipts are rejected;
- older rosters are ignored and locally blocked members stay out;
- group creation needs camera or motion input;
- held reserve keys survive vault encoding.

These are unit tests, not a security audit. Not run: assembleDebug, lint and device tests (no app
code changed).

and SYSTEM_DESIGN.md read before development.

something"); joining instructions named options differently from the plain-text option list;
messaging had too many options; received files had to be saved to the device to be seen.

Implemented:
- NFC: Android drops reader mode whenever the activity pauses (e.g. QR scanner); the recipient
  then read the owner as an "Empty tag", whose system screen backgrounded and locked Enigma.
  NearbyJoinDialog re-arms reader mode on every MainActivity.onResume while waiting; the owner
  disables its own NFC polling with setDiscoveryTechnology on Android 15+ while serving and
  resets it on every exit. NFC read failures now show the underlying reason.

  on-screen instructions and the recipient's Scan QR code button.
- Messages: three tabs (Home removed); conversation list per group+person (Inbox); Send = type a
  message or choose a file, then group, then person, Encrypt & share (typed text is a file-v3
  .enigma package named message.txt, text/plain); Send as text instead keeps QKD3; Receive =
  Open .enigma file or paste text, group/sender found from the packet; Enigma accepts
  ACTION_VIEW/ACTION_SEND content:// files and text from other apps after unlock.
- In-app viewer: images (<=25 MiB, subsampled to <=2048 px) and text (<=1 MiB) restored into a
  zeroed in-memory buffer after full verification and a durable replay-ID commit; other types
  show name/size. Save to device keeps fresh passphrase authentication.
