Earlier 0.4 evidence covered scanner launch/cancel, not actual QR camera decoding
or enrolled biometric unlock. These additional regressions now cover an emulator;
physical-phone fixes still require the owner's confirmation.

Limits: static identity keys/no forward secrecy or ratchet; no automatic group
rekey/owner transfer/recovery; local removal does not revoke retained secrets;
TTL uses clocks/client policy and cannot erase saved plaintext. Biometric unlock
is an alternate route, not a second factor. RSA identity/device password-box key
are not per-operation user-auth bound. Unlocked endpoint compromise remains a risk.
The protected vault is local app storage, not a dedicated personal file-storage feature.
Old formats do not gain new group-secret protection; compatibility is not a DEV promise.
